#  Healthcare EDA

Exploratory and statistical analysis of two healthcare datasets, using visualization and hypothesis testing to uncover trends and group differences.

## Analyses

- **Heart health** (`HeartHealth.ipynb`)
- **Body performance** (`EDA+ | Exploratory & Statistical Analysis- Body Performance.ipynb`):

## Approach

- Data exploration and visualization of distributions, trends, and relationships
- Statistical testing with **t-tests** and **ANOVA** to check whether group differences are significant
- Summarizing insights to support data-driven decisions

## Key findings

- *[A strong correlation coefficient between Systolic and Diastolic blood pressure is observed, indicating a significant relationship between these two variables.]*

- *[There is noticeable increase in chance of heart disease beyond age of 42]*

- *[The positive cases peaks higher than negative cases around 60-90 mmHg, indicating a higher concentration of individuals diagnosed with a heart issue in this pressure range]*
